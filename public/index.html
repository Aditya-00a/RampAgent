How it works

From raw transactions to board-ready risk.

Three stages turn a stream of card swipes into exposure, a risk register, and control-effectiveness ratings — no sampling, no spreadsheets.

Step 1

Detect

An ensemble of six ML / statistical detectors plus LLM policy testing screens every transaction for anomalies, duplicates, off-hours activity, and policy breaches.

Step 2

Score

Signals combine into one risk score (anomaly×0.55 + policy×0.45) mapped LOW→CRITICAL. Expected loss weights each flagged dollar by its probability.

Step 3

Govern

Scores roll up into a risk register (likelihood × impact), KRIs tracked against appetite, and control-effectiveness ratings — the artifacts an audit team acts on.

Continuous, not periodic

Replace sample-based audit with always-on monitoring.

  • 100% coverageEvery transaction tested against every control — not a 5–10% sample reviewed weeks later.
  • Quantified exposureDollars at risk and probability-weighted expected loss, prioritised by severity.
  • Audit-ready evidenceAn immutable event log and AI-written narratives for every flagged item.
  • Ask in plain EnglishA built-in AI risk analyst answers questions about exposure, controls, and the register.
