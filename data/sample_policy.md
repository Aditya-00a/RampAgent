# Acme Corp — Corporate Expense Policy

_Effective January 1, 2024 | Finance Department_

## 1. Meals & Dining

- **Solo meals:** Maximum $25 per person during business hours
- **Team meals:** Maximum $40 per person for team lunches/dinners
- **Client entertainment meals:** Maximum $75 per person; requires client name in description
- **All meals over $100 total** require manager pre-approval
- Alcohol is permitted only for client entertainment, capped at $30 per person

## 2. Travel — Flights

- Economy class is required for domestic flights under 5 hours
- **Flights over $500** require pre-approval from department head
- Bookings must be made at least 7 days in advance when possible
- Last-minute bookings (under 48 hours) require VP-level approval

## 3. Travel — Hotels

- **Maximum nightly rate:** $250 per night (domestic), $300 per night (international)
- Extended stays (4+ nights) require pre-approval
- Room service and minibar charges are not reimbursable

## 4. Travel — Ground Transportation

- Rideshare (Uber/Lyft) is preferred over taxis when available
- **Maximum per ride:** $75 for standard trips
- Airport transfers up to $100 are auto-approved
- Car rentals require pre-approval

## 5. Software & SaaS

- **Purchases under $50:** Auto-approved for Engineering department
- **Purchases $50–$200:** Require manager approval
- **Purchases over $200:** Require IT department sign-off and manager approval
- Annual renewals over $500 must go through procurement

## 6. Office Supplies

- **Individual purchases up to $100:** Auto-approved
- **Purchases $100–$300:** Require manager approval
- **Purchases over $300:** Require department head approval
- Personal items (decor, non-work accessories) are not reimbursable

## 7. Entertainment

- **Maximum $100 per person** for client or team entertainment
- Requires business justification in the expense description
- Weekend entertainment requires pre-approval
- **No personal entertainment** on the corporate card

## 8. General Rules

- **Receipts are required** for all transactions over $25
- All expenses must have a clear business purpose in the description
- **No personal expenses** on the corporate card under any circumstances
- Split transactions to avoid approval thresholds are strictly prohibited
- Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the transaction date
- **Transactions between 11:00 PM and 5:00 AM** are automatically flagged for review
- Duplicate charges must be reported within 48 hours
